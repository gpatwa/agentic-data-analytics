>
> Hello,
>
> We build a governed natural-language analytics product and would like to use the NYC TLC trip-record data and the NYC 311 service-request data as reference datasets in a hosted demo, where visitors could ask questions of a copy of the data. Before we do, could you confirm:
>
> 1. Which terms of use or licence apply to redistributing a copy of these datasets (or a derived, aggregated view) on a commercial service?
> 2. Whether attribution is required, and in what form.
> 3. Whether there are restrictions on the free-text and location fields in the 311 data.
>
> We would link to the source and describe the data as it is published. Thank you.
